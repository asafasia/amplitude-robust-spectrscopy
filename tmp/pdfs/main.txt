   Amplitude-Robust Qubit Spectroscopy Using Echo-Root-Lorentzian Pulse Shapes

                                          Asaf A. Solonnikov and Nadav Katz
                       The Racah Institute of Physics, The Hebrew University of Jerusalem, Israel
                                                 (Dated: July 19, 2026)
                We present a qubit spectroscopy method based on Lorentzian-derived drive pulses that enables
             T2 -limited frequency characterization at drive amplitudes exceeding those used in conventional spec-
             troscopy by more than three orders of magnitude, thereby enhancing robustness against amplitude
             variations. Specifically, we employ a root-Lorentzian envelope with a π-phase inversion at its peak,
             which introduces an echo-like symmetry that cancels on-resonant Rabi oscillations while preserving
             the spectral response. This approach suppresses conventional power broadening and enables high-
             resolution spectroscopy using pulses shorter than durations required by standard techniques. We
             experimentally demonstrate this method on a superconducting qubit platform and observe excel-
             lent agreement with numerical simulations, highlighting its efficiency and reliability for rapid and
             calibration-robust qubit frequency characterization.
