Fit definition and selected-trace details

Every spectrum was fit independently to a Gaussian dip plus a quadratic background. A smoothly tapered, curvature-scaled window and a robust residual loss reduce the influence of weak shoulders. No smoothing across amplitude or cutoff is applied.

The reported width and contrast describe the central Gaussian component. Rejected fits are excluded from the map and metric cards; when available, their candidate curves are shown in red for diagnosis.

The report selects saved simulations and fits; changing a control does not run a new simulation or optimize a new fit. Original spectra remain unchanged. Embedded population display copies use float32; fit parameters retain float64 precision.
