In conclusion, we have demonstrated a narrow spectroscopic depletion feature
that persists over more than two decades of peak drive. Measurements
establish its linewidth, contrast, and drive-induced center motion, while
numerical calculations show how DRAG and polynomial edge correction can improve high-drive linewidth and probability contrast. The experiment--simulation comparison in
Fig.~\ref{fig:robust-echo-spectroscopy} supports the echo interpretation,
while the duration--cutoff scan identifies the competing linewidth and
contrast requirements. Phase-inverted shaped pulses thus provide a route
to high-resolution qubit spectroscopy beyond weak drive.
