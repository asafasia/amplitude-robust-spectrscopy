In conclusion, we have demonstrated a narrow spectroscopic depletion feature
across a wide drive range. The completed measurements establish its
linewidth, contrast, and observed center motion, and show that polynomial
shaping with DRAG improves the measured response relative to DRAG alone.
The corrected median linewidth remains above the measured Ramsey limit.
The reference simulations support the echo interpretation, while the
numerical duration--cutoff scan identifies competing linewidth and
contrast requirements. Phase-inverted shaped pulses thus provide a route
to amplitude-robust qubit spectroscopy beyond weak drive.
