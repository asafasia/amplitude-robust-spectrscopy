\title{Amplitude-Robust Qubit Spectroscopy Using Echo-Root-Lorentzian Pulse Shapes}

\author{Asaf A. Solonnikov and Nadav Katz}
    % \email[Correspondence email address: ]{asaf.solonnikov@mail.huji.ac.il}% Your name
    \affiliation{The Racah institute of physics, The Hebrew University of Jerusalem, Israel}

\date{\today} % Leave empty to omit a date

\begin{abstract}

We present a qubit spectroscopy method based on Lorentzian-derived drive pulses that enables $T_2$-limited frequency characterization at drive amplitudes exceeding those used in conventional spectroscopy by more than three orders of magnitude, thereby enhancing robustness against amplitude variations. Specifically, we employ a root-Lorentzian envelope with a $\pi$-phase inversion at its peak, which introduces an echo-like symmetry that cancels on-resonant Rabi oscillations while preserving the spectral response. This approach suppresses conventional power broadening and enables high-resolution spectroscopy using pulses shorter than durations required by standard techniques. We experimentally demonstrate this method on a superconducting qubit platform and observe excellent agreement with numerical simulations, highlighting its efficiency and reliability for rapid and calibration-robust qubit frequency characterization.

\end{abstract}
