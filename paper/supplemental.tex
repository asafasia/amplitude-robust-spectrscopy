\setcounter{secnumdepth}{2}

This Supplemental Material provides the experimental setup and qubit coherence
characterization; a precise definition of the finite echo-root-Lorentzian
pulse and its resonant cancellation mechanism; the Bloch-equation coherence
limit; pulse-cutoff and adiabaticity analyses; the numerical model and
numerical protocol comparisons; and a dense pulse-length campaign with
matched three-level linewidth comparisons. The AC-Stark discussion distinguishes
the dressed-state shift and a phase-average estimate from the finite-pulse
spectral center.
The closing two sections examine pulse modifications---the midpoint phase
inversion, DRAG quadrature, and cutoff-edge smoothing---and then extend the
cutoff--duration study of the polynomial-corrected DRAG echo to the full
cutoff range, including the linewidth--contrast tradeoff and numerical checks.
