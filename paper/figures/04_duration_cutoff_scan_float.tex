    linewidth values above 10 saturate the color scale.
    (c),(d) Cutoff slices at $\Omega_0/(2\pi)=49.8~\mathrm{MHz}$
    (orange dashed lines) for durations $L=10$, 20, 30, 40, and $50~\mu\mathrm{s}$.
