\section{Comparison Between Simulation and Experiment}
\label{sec:simulation-experiment-comparison}

Simulation and experiment are compared using the same final-state
spectroscopic observable and the same linewidth-extraction procedure.  The
archived curves in the present figures use the historical coherence inputs
described in Sec.~\ref{sec:numerical-model}, rather than the independently
measured q1 values.  Their agreement with the data is therefore interpreted
qualitatively as support for the echo-cancellation mechanism, not as a
parameter-matched validation.  A quantitative comparison requires regenerating
the simulations with $T_1=\MeasuredTOne~\mu\mathrm{s}$ and either the
conservative Ramsey reference
$T_{2,\mathrm{eff}}=\EffectiveTTwo~\mu\mathrm{s}$ or the later measured
Hahn-echo value $T_2=\MeasuredTTwoEcho~\mu\mathrm{s}$, with the choice matched
to the acquisition campaign.  The June 28 comparisons deliberately use the
former.  Systematic deviations at the largest amplitudes can also
reflect physics omitted from the effective model, including higher-level
coupling and strong-drive calibration effects.
