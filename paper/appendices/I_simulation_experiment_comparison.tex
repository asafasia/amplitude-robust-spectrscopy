\section{Comparison Between Simulation and Experiment}
\label{sec:simulation-experiment-comparison}

The retained simulations in main-text Fig.~2 match the pulse duration and
cutoff but use the historical model inputs
$T_1=\MeasuredTOne~\mu\mathrm{s}$ and $T_2=\EffectiveTTwoDisplay~\mu\mathrm{s}$.
The new measured maps instead have representative measured values
$T_1=\LatestTwoTOne~\mu\mathrm{s}$ and
$T_2^*=\LatestTwoTTwoStar~\mu\mathrm{s}$, and different sampling grids.
The comparison therefore supports the echo-cancellation mechanism
qualitatively; it is not a new parameter-matched validation.
A quantitative comparison would require the campaign-specific sampled
waveforms, coherence inputs and readout observable. Two-state experimental
discrimination does not separately resolve the simulated $|f\rangle$
population. Updated main-text Fig.~3 is an independent experimental
comparison, with its own representative coherence reference.
The archived supplemental comparisons retain the inputs documented alongside
each dataset. Strong-drive discrepancies can also reflect higher-level
coupling and calibration effects beyond the effective model.
